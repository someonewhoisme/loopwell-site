Privacy policy

Loopwell is an iPhone app for finding short-lived groups ("Loops") within 1 km of where you are, and chatting with the people there. This policy explains what Loopwell collects, why, and how long it keeps it.

Who can see what

  • Loops and their chats are visible to signed-in Loopwell users who are within 1 km of them.
  • Other people never see your location. Your position on the map is shown only to you.
  • If you block someone, you won't see their content and they won't see yours.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts Loopwell's database and accounts.
  • Apple distributes the app and provides maps and place search on your device.

Loopwell has no ads, doesn't sell your data, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open the Account tab and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, display name, Loops, messages, reports you made and blocks. It can't be undone.

Children

Loopwell isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, email loopwell.support@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Questions about privacy: loopwell.support@gmail.com.

Community guidelines

Everyone agrees to these rules before using Loopwell. There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech.
  • No sexual content, violence or illegal activity.
  • No spam, scams or impersonating someone else.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information without permission.

To report something, long-press a message, or use the ••• menu on a Loop, and choose Report. You can also Block the person. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; content or accounts that break these rules are removed. Anything reported by three or more people is hidden automatically until it's reviewed.

Support

Questions, a problem with the app, or something to report that you can't report in the app? Email us and we'll reply within two business days.

loopwell.support@gmail.com

Common questions

  • Why don't I see any Loops? Loops only appear within 1 km of you and last 1 hour. Make sure Location is allowed in Settings → Loopwell → Location, then pull down to refresh.
  • Where did my messages go? Messages disappear 15 minutes after they're sent.
  • How do I delete my account? Account tab → Delete Account.
